01 · Growth

Growth comes from price, not from more pieces

From 2019 to 2025 net revenue per piece rose 5.1×. Units rose 2.1×. In 2026 so far the gap continues: revenue +32.6%, pieces +8.1%.

If price increases slow down, revenue growth slows with them. Volume is the part Cottonil controls.

    04 · Discount

    Every discount point comes out of margin

    Lines sold at full price keep 41.0% gross margin. Lines discounted 15–25% keep 29.2%. Returns stay flat across bands, so deep discounts buy no extra loyalty in this data.

    Capping discounts at 10% would have kept 1.84M EGP over the last 12 months, if volume held.

    05 · Costs

    Profit improved because costs grew slower than sales

    Gross margin stayed near 38.3% every year. Operating costs fell from 29.1% of net in 2019 to 13.6% in 2025, which lifted the EBITDA proxy from 6.3% to 18.8%.

    This only holds while revenue keeps outgrowing costs.

    08 · How it's built

    One database, four workflows, every number traceable

    The raw extract was loaded once into Supabase (Postgres with pgvector). The dashboard, the workflows and the assistant all read the same checked tables, so a number means the same thing everywhere. Hover a box to follow its lines.

    Why Supabase

    • Numbers and knowledge in one place. Sales tables, certified views and the assistant's knowledge base (pgvector) live in the same Postgres, so the assistant never mixes two versions of the truth.
    • Permissions in the database, not in the prompt. The assistant's role assistant_ro can read the 9 certified views and nothing else: no raw tables, no customer names, no writes.
    • An API n8n can call. Workflows call small SQL functions over the Supabase API. No database password and no key sit in this page.

    What keeps the numbers honest

    • Checked on load. Orders, pieces and net revenue in Supabase match an independent recount of the raw files (28 of 28 headline numbers).
    • Every answer has a query. The assistant must take each number from a SQL result; an answer with an unbacked number is blocked, not shown.
    • Tested every week. A 19-question test set runs every Sunday and fails below 90%. Every run and every question is logged.
